About our restaurant

A welcoming taste of Nilore.

Welcome to Appetizer Fast Foods — your neighbourhood destination for great taste in Nilore, Islamabad. Located on Lehtrar Road, we bring together beloved Pakistani street-food flavours, hearty burgers and shawarma, light chaats and samosas, and cooling milkshakes and refreshments. Whether you're stopping by with friends, grabbing a quick bite, or refreshing your day with a chilled drink, you'll find a welcoming, clean and friendly place to enjoy fresh bites made with care. We focus on variety, flavour and convenient WhatsApp ordering, so your favourites are always just a message away.
